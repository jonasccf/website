Hi,
I’m Jonas Ferdinand.

I’m a sociologist studying how digital capitalism transforms work, culture, and subjectivity.

I’m a visiting researcher in the research group »Globalization, Work, and Production« at the WZB Berlin Social Science Center and an associated researcher in the group »Working with Artificial Intelligence« at the Weizenbaum Institute. My focus is digital capitalism – in particular the platform economy and the transformation of work in the digitalized cultural and creative industries.

01 Research

The central question driving my work: how are platforms, data, and AI changing what work is – and who gets paid for it?

Platform economy

From Roblox to TikTok, platforms blur the line between play, creativity, and unpaid labor. I study these new forms of work – such as the phenomenon of »playbour« in the video game industry.

Work in the creative industries

How precarious is creative work in the digital economy – and what can public funding achieve? Case studies from the video game, fashion, and social media industries.

AI and work

Under what conditions do employees and works councils accept technologies such as wearables and AI in the workplace – and what does AI mean for democracy in the world of work?
